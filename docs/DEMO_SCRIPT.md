# Three-minute video storyboard

Profile pages first, then the rankings, with the agents actually dating in between.

## 0:00–0:20 — What it is

Open `/`. One sentence: every person is represented by an agent that reads their LinkedIn and Instagram, dates the other agents, and ranks who fits them best.

## 0:20–0:55 — Pasting the links in

Open **Add people**. Paste one LinkedIn link and one public Instagram link, tick the box, click **Read profiles & build profile page**. The site reads both pages live and lands on the profile page.

## 0:55–1:35 — How the agent analyzes a person

On the profile page: the two source cards (expand **What the agent read**), then the summary, **Needs**, **Hobbies**, **Interests**, and the other qualities (values, personality, lifestyle, communication). Point at the quoted evidence under each claim.

## 1:35–2:20 — The agents dating

Open **Demo → Watch two agents date** (or **Add people → Start dating** to show the live progress bar across all 300 dates). Read a few turns aloud: one agent shares, the other reacts and asks about a need. Scroll to the two "agent reports back" verdicts.

## 2:20–2:50 — Rankings

Open **Rankings**, pick a person: rank 1…24 with the agent's verdict, why it may work, friction, and a link to the date. Open a second person to show the ranking is personal.

## 2:50–3:00 — Close

Back on **Demo**: 25 real people, 300 dates, 600 ranking rows — all from two public links per person.
